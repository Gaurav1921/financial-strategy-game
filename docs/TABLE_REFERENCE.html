Hostile Ledger

Everything a table needs in view during a live game. Keep this page visible throughout. The Power Cards section at the bottom is meant to be cut apart and dealt privately, one card per player, not read by the whole table.

Everyone starts identical. Pick a company name, an icon, and one Industry. A minority of the table is secretly a Raider — everyone still starts at 100 Power either way. 15 rounds total: a Building Phase (4–6 rounds, length hidden, no Hostile Bids) then the Conflict Phase (Hostile Bids open) through Round 15, the Final Round, which plays by different rules — expect honest play to end there.

The Round Loop every round, same 8 steps

  1. Income — Company, Real Estate, Gold, Market positions pay out automatically.
  2. Allocate — the one real decision: Company, Real Estate, Gold, the Market, a Bank deposit, a loan paydown, or hold cash.
  3. Alliances — propose or accept a Joint Venture; existing ones resolve.
  4. Loans — borrow if you want, Bank capacity permitting; peer loans settle.
  5. Combat — Hostile Bids and "gang up on the leader" counter-attacks (Conflict Phase only).
  6. The Market re-prices — Industry positions revalue against this round's scenario.
  7. Taxes & upkeep — if enabled: income tax, Real Estate liquidation, idle cash erosion.
  8. Round ends — move to the next.

Round Timer

3 minutes per round, ending early the moment everyone signals ready. Miss the timer and your round holds: your split repeats exactly what you did last round, nothing new goes out on your behalf. Miss a round entirely and look genuinely gone? Any other player can call a vote after your first miss; a majority of the table's other active players sends you to the Board Observer track — your holdings freeze exactly where they were, but stay completely real and fully attackable. Reconnect and you're straight back in, no re-vote needed.

What You Can See

Only Power is shown, exactly, always. Everything else — cash, Company, Real Estate, Gold, debt, exact attack or defense strength — is unknown unless it goes through a Declaration (a claim about your own position) that anyone can Audit.

Audit cost5 cash, paid by the auditor
Claim was trueauditor's 5 cash goes to the claimant
Claim was a lie, caught~15% of the liar's Power, docked on the spot; effect voided; auditor refunded

Combat

Attack power = your cash × a portion, plus allies backing you
Defense = Real Estate ×0.9 + Gold ×0.6 + cash ×0.3, plus declared allies' cash ×0.3
  • Ties favor the defender (defense counts at ×1.05).
  • Hostile Bid (Conflict Phase only): on success, captures 25% of the target's total wealth — cash, then Company, then Real Estate, then Gold. Target keeps their company and returns behind a 2-round Post-Bid Shield.
  • Failed attack: attacker loses 50% of what they staked, split between the defender and the Bank.
  • Gang up on the leader: anyone can pile on once a leader's Power exceeds 1.05× second place, not just dedicated attackers.

Alliances

Joint Venture (financial): each partner seeds 10 into one Industry, the pot moves with that Industry's real scenario delta every round. Either partner can top up (20% of whichever partner has less cash, floored at 10) or drain early: drainer keeps 65%, partner gets 35% (40/40 if both drain, 50/50 if neither). A second proven drain costs the drainer a visible ~15% Power hit on the spot.

Defense Pact (protective): declared visibly boosts your partner's defense; covert stays hidden until a fight happens. Ending a covert pact is free. Ending a declared pact is free the first time, costs a visible ~15% Power hit the second time, and takes effect starting next round. One JV/Pact partner at a time, two allies max — it's the same relationship, not two separate slots.

Power Cards — deal privately, one per player

Each player secretly holds one of these 7 cards. You only ever need to know your own. Claiming a card's action or block is a Declaration — anyone can Audit it. Each half (action, block) works once per game.

The Financier

Action · Capital Raise

Any round. Add 20% of your current Company value straight to your Cash.

Block · Freeze

Any round, named target. Zero that target's Company and Real Estate income this round.

The Marauder

Action · Smash and Grab

Conflict Phase only. Capture 10% of a named target's total wealth straight to your Cash — smaller than a full Hostile Bid, no shield triggered.

The Guardian

Block · Bodyguard

Conflict Phase only. Fully negate one attack against your own company — a Hostile Bid, a counter-attack, or a Marauder's hit.

The Broker

Action · Skim

Conflict Phase only. Take 8% of a named target's current Cash directly — a pickpocket, smaller even than a Marauder hit.

Block · Vault

Any round. Fully negate one Skim attempt against you.

The Banker

Action · Easy Terms

Any round. Take a Bank loan at the flat 8% base rate, no leverage premium.

Block · Standstill

Any round. Your next interest charge skips the leverage premium, and you can't be denied a loan for lack of Bank capacity.

The Insider

Action · Tip-Off

Any round, named target. Privately learn their true Cash, Company, Real Estate, Gold, and Debt. No block exists for this card.

The Analyst

Action · Report or Expose

Any round, named target, pick one: Report shifts their visible defense +/-15% for the round, or Expose reveals their Market positions to the table.

Block · Countermeasure

Any round. Fully negate a Report or Expose aimed at you.
